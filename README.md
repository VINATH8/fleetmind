# FleetMind

### Autonomous Financial Intelligence for EV Fleet Operations

> **Predict demand. Detect risk. Optimize deployment. Maximize contribution margin.**

FleetMind is an AI-powered fleet intelligence and decision-support platform built for electric vehicle and scooter fleet operators.

Instead of optimizing a fleet purely around ride volume or utilization, FleetMind focuses on a more important operational question:

> **"What fleet action will create the highest financial contribution while accounting for demand, battery, vehicle health, operational costs, payment success, and failure risk?"**

FleetMind combines:

- Machine Learning
- AI Agents
- Demand Forecasting
- Fleet Risk Analysis
- Battery Intelligence
- Maintenance Prioritization
- Financial Modeling
- Counterfactual Simulation
- Interactive Analytics

into a single fleet operations interface.

# 🚨 Problem

Managing a large EV fleet is not simply a matter of putting vehicles where demand is highest.

A fleet operator has to simultaneously consider:

- Where demand will occur
- How many vehicles are available
- Battery levels
- Vehicle health
- Maintenance requirements
- Failure probability
- Relocation costs
- Energy costs
- Expected revenue
- Payment success
- Operational risk

A naive deployment strategy might say:

> "Demand is high in this zone, so move as many scooters as possible."

But this can be financially inefficient.

Moving additional scooters may increase the number of potential rides while also increasing:

- relocation cost
- energy consumption
- operational overhead
- failure exposure
- maintenance requirements

Eventually, adding more scooters can reduce the overall contribution margin.

FleetMind is designed to identify this point automatically.
